A two-band fit to $W3–W4$ alone requires a cooler component ($T_{\text{eff,dust}} \approx 305\text{ K}$, $289–326\text{ K}$, conditional luminosity $\sim 1.7\%$), which supplies only `0.28 mJy` at $W2$—requiring multi-temperature circumstellar material, warm silicate/PAH emission features, or a non-stellar component to account for both $W1–W2$ and $W3–W4$.
- **Channel 6 (`generic_outlier`) Caveat**: `generic_outlier` shares input features (`chi_w4`, `ks_w3`, `ir_var_chi2`) with `ir_excess` and `ir_timedomain` and therefore **does not constitute a statistically independent confirmation**; reported `1.0e-12` FAP values reflect the analytic/numerical calibration floor rather than empirical tail sampling beyond $N \le 10^3$.

### Abstract *(Draft)*
