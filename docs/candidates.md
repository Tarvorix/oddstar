### 3.3 Time-Domain Behavior & Astrometric Shear Falsification Test (2010–2024)

2. **2014–2024 NEOWISE-R Light Curve & Proper-Motion Shear Test (21 Visits, 283 Frames)**:
   - Channel 2 (`ir_timedomain`): `chi2_w1 = 13.62`, `chi2_w2 = 41.19`, `slope_snr = 10.77`, `max_jump_snr = 8.16`, `color_change_mag = 0.197`, `n_visits = 21`.
   - From the `2014–2016` quiescent baseline ($W1 \approx 10.630$, $W2 \approx 10.264$) to the `2017–2021` peak ($W1 = 10.406 \pm 0.010$ at MJD `58493.7`, $W2 = 9.871 \pm 0.017$ at MJD `59224.9`), the isolated flare component ($\Delta W1 \approx 3.97\text{ mJy}$ / $12.23\text{ mag}$, $\Delta W2 \approx 5.88\text{ mJy}$ / $11.16\text{ mag}$) exhibits a very red color $(W1 - W2)_{\text{flare}} \approx +1.06\text{ mag}$ before fading back toward baseline across `2022–2024` ($W2 = 10.162–10.210$). This strongly disfavors a stationary background AGN displaced by `> ~0.2"–0.3"`, though a subarcsecond blend or flux-dependent PSF bias cannot be excluded at WISE resolution.

## 4. Research Paper Section (Living Draft)

> **Working Title**: *A Reproducible Multi-Channel Search for Mid-Infrared and Time-Domain Outliers Across the Local Thick Disk, All-Sky Galactic Halo, and Galactic Bulge*

### Abstract *(Draft)*
We present `oddstar`, a reproducible, provenance-tracked archival survey pipeline enforcing pre-registered frozen thresholds (`config_hash = ce6e224fd31d`) and explicit tri-state vetting (`pass`, `fail`, `not_evaluated`), across **1,800 Gaia DR3 stars** cross-matched with 2MASS and AllWISE in three kinematic and spatial regimes: a 300-star local thick-disk/halo sample ($v_{\text{tan}} > 65\text{ km s}^{-1}$), a 1,000-star random all-sky metal-poor Halo sample ($[\text{Fe/H}] \le -0.8$, $v_{\text{tan}} > 150\text{ km s}^{-1}$, `759` HEALPix cells), and a 500-star Galactic Bulge sample ($|l| \le 12^\circ$, $d = 2.23–10.56\text{ kpc}$). Searching three channels (`ir_excess`, `underluminous`, `generic_outlier`) yields **zero surviving unvetted candidates across all 1,500 Halo and Bulge stars**, establishing 95% confidence null upper limits at 90% sensitivity of $\le 0.332\%$ ($N_{\text{eff}} = 900$) in the all-sky Halo and $\le 0.664\%$ ($N_{\text{eff}} = 450$) in the Galactic Bulge, after resolving all initial Halo/Bulge flags as WISE diffraction/upper-limit artifacts, crowded blends (`up to 22` Gaia neighbours), background optical galaxies (`PS1 DR2`), long-period variables, or known metal-poor subdwarfs including `G 251-44 / LHS 236` (`Gaia DR3 1111149103659931264`, `sdK7`). In the 300-star local box (`UL <= 1.745%`), a single target remains at **Tier C**: **`Gaia DR3 5628404318169472`** (`AllWISE J025411.56+055258.2`; $d = 231.76\text{ pc}$, $v_{\text{tan}} = 114.60\text{ km s}^{-1}$, $T_{\text{eff}} = 4510\text{ K}$). Anchoring a $4510\text{ K}$ photosphere to $K_s = 10.971$ confirms mid-IR excesses of $5.54\times$ at $12\,\mu\text{m}$ and $18.31\times$ at $22\,\mu\text{m}$, plus a coherent `2017–2021` NEOWISE-R brightening ($\Delta W2 \approx -0.41\text{ mag}$, also catalogued in VarWISE cluster `2540580983592406`) whose centroid tracks Gaia proper motion across 21 visits with `0.053"` median residual. Ground-based ZTF and ASAS-SN monitoring exclude a sustained `~0.4 mag` optical brightening. Because a single blackbody cannot match both the flat $12–22\,\mu\text{m}$ spectrum ($F_{\nu,W3}/F_{\nu,W4} \approx 0.90$) and the `W1/W2` flare, and `4` vetting checks (`second_instrument`, faint-star RVS `binary`, `pipeline_agreement`, programmatic `second_survey`) remain open, subarcsecond high-resolution imaging and $5–25\,\mu\text{m}$ spectroscopy are required to distinguish extreme circumstellar collision debris from a subarcsecond obscured blend.

### 2. Candidate `Gaia DR3 5628404318169472`
Combining Gaia DR3, 2MASS, AllWISE (19 cryogenic exposures), Pan-STARRS DR2 (`r_PSF - r_Kron = -0.001`), NEOWISE-R (`21` visits), ZTF, ASAS-SN, and TESS establishes that `Gaia DR3 5628404318169472` is an astrometrically single ($\text{RUWE} = 1.078$, `non_single_star = 0`), solar-metallicity ($[\text{M/H}] = +0.036$) K4V star ($M_G = 6.532$, $T_{\text{eff}} = 4510\text{ K}$) moving with $v_{\text{tan}} = 114.60\text{ km s}^{-1}$. Anchoring a $4510\text{ K}$ photosphere to $K_s = 10.971$ confirms a $5.54\times$ ($W3$) and $18.31\times$ ($W4$) excess in 2010, and coherent `2017–2021` `W1/W2` brightening ($\Delta W2 \approx -0.41\text{ mag}$, VarWISE cluster `2540580983592406`) followed by fading toward baseline in `2022–2024`. Fitting the NEOWISE-R visit centroids across 2014–2024 reproduces the Gaia proper motion with `0.053"` median residual (`0.051"` at peak vs. `0.054"` in quiescence) while the star moves `1.41"` relative to its 2010 coordinates, disfavoring displaced (`> 0.2"–0.3"`) background extragalactic sources. Simultaneously, ZTF (`0.055–0.060 mag` yearly median span) and differential ASAS-SN (`0.065 mag` `g`-band span) exclude a sustained `~0.4 mag` optical brightening.

### 3. Open Physical Interpretations & Required Observations
The naive single `585.8 K`, `4.4%` blackbody model fails to match the flat `12–22 um` spectrum ($\Delta W3/\Delta W4 \approx 0.90$, where a two-band fit yields `~305 K` and `~1.7%` conditional luminosity), and Gaia RVS (`rv_method_used=2`) leaves close SB1 companions unconstrained. Three observations will settle `Gaia DR3 5628404318169472`:
1. **High-resolution mid-IR imaging & $5–25\,\mu\text{m}$ spectroscopy** (e.g., JWST MIRI) to verify spatial coincidence below `0.3"` and test whether `2010` $W3/W4$ emission faded alongside `W1/W2` and exhibits warm silicate debris features vs. a subarcsecond background torus.
2. **Optical spectroscopy & multi-epoch RV monitoring** (`R >= 20,000`) to test H$\alpha$ activity, Li I $\lambda 6708$ youth, spectral subtype, and binarity across orbital phases.
3. **Matched-detrending TESS / AO NIR imaging** to close `pipeline_agreement` and bound faint subarcsecond optical/NIR companions.
